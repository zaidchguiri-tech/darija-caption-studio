How to use

1. Upload
Drag & drop or click the upload area. Any audio or video file works.
2. Transcribe
Audio is sent to OpenAI Whisper. Moroccan Darija captions are generated with timestamps.
3. Edit
Click any caption to edit. Adjust start/end times in the time fields.
4. Sync
The active caption highlights automatically as audio plays.
5. Translate
Pick Arabic, French, or English and click Translate — powered by Gemini.
6. Export
Download as SRT, VTT, TXT, or JSON.
